Asset-Light Digital Intermediary for India

Bridging India's Informal Waste Economy to Formal EPR Markets

Project Sanchay empowers informal electronic waste aggregators (kabadiwalas) to list materials, enables certified recyclers to accept handovers, and creates tamper-evident digital manifests that provide electronics brands with traceable ESG supply-chain transparency.

Sanchay 5-Stage Chain of Custody Workflow

1

Collector Lists

Kabadiwala lists collected circuit boards, phones, or components with asking price and location.

2

Recycler Reserves

Authorized recycler finds listing in marketplace and reserves it through atomic database transaction.

3

Physical Weighing

Waste is weighed on calibrated scale; actual weight recorded and scale slip photos uploaded.

4

Dual Confirmation

Both parties digitally sign the handover. System automatically issues Digital Manifest with QR.

5

Brand Dashboard

Electronics OEMs view verified downstream volumes, review manifests, and export audit data.
